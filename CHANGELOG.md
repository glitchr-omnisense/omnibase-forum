# Changelog

## 2.0.0

omnibase/forum: a bulletin board for an omnibase site - boards in groups, topics and their posts, polls, tags, scheduled topics, the moderators' tools - on omnisense/omnibase 7.0's agnostic repositories (the finder language, the counts per board and per tag included as findValuesBy() with aggregates, each query written once and compiled by omnibridge/doctrine or omnibridge/eloquent), its lists paged by the core's Paginator, its writes through the unit of work.
